# Handoff to sales

What the account executive, or the CEO on a founder-led deal, receives with every booked demo. Seven fields, written during or right after the call, attached to the Demo booked status. Filled example in `skills/demo-booking-call/references/attempts-and-handoff.md`.

| # | Field | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who | Name, role, authority proof (registry mandate, or tenure ratio with the threshold it crossed), who signs above them |
| 2 | Why now | The signal and its date, the deadline or season behind it |
| 3 | Pain in their words | Verbatim, two lines maximum |
| 4 | Stack detected | Incumbent tools from stack-detection, confirmed or corrected on the call |
| 5 | Objections raised | Which ones, and the response that landed |
| 6 | Agreed next step | What the demo will show, on whose data, in how long |
| 7 | Calendar confirmation | Invitation accepted on the call; day-before (T-1) reminder scheduled with the data request |

An empty field is filled or marked "not obtained" with the reason. "Not obtained" on field 1 means the demo may need a second attendee on the buyer side; the account executive decides before the demo, not after.

What the demo team should not have to do: research the company, guess who decides, find out what tool is in place, or discover the objection live. If any of those happen, the handoff failed and the failure is logged in the weekly review.
