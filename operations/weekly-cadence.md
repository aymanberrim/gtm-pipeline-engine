# Cadence

The channel runs on three clocks. Detail, minute by minute, in `skills/channel-operating-cadence/references/daily-weekly-monthly-checklists.md`.

## Daily, 15 minutes, before 09:30

1. Open the three safety-net views. Empty them: assign the next action and date to every row. Log what let each row in.
2. Read the two circuit breakers: open rate and reply rate on the trailing window. Below 35% or 3%, the campaign is paused before anything else happens.
3. Purchase the day's mobile batch for the To-call queue: in-ICP leads with a real signal only, within the daily cap, highest score first.
4. Confirm tomorrow's demos (day-before (T-1) reminder with the data request).

## Weekly, 60 minutes, Friday

1. Per-group reply and positive-reply rates. A group under 3% reply after 60 sends goes back to segmentation; a group at 8% reply with at least 1 positive per 25 sends is scaled.
2. The funnel, step by step: Captured → Enriched → In ICP → In sequence → Signal → Reached → Qualified → Demo booked → Demo held. Pick the leakiest step and ship one fix.
3. Demos booked vs held; any no-show gets a root cause.
4. Win/loss notes from stage 6 into the ICP rule, the battlecard and the group angles.
5. Domain health and deliverability log.
6. Cost per demo, computed from invoices and a timesheet.
7. Write the one-page CEO report: five numbers, what changed, what was fixed, what is next.

## Monthly, half a day

- ICP refresh from won and lost deals.
- Stack-detection sweep of accounts added in the month.
- Market-entry scorecard re-check on the chosen country; entry-mode switch triggers reviewed.
- Compliance pack refresh (sub-processor list, conformity declarations, risk memo).
- Idempotence test: re-run scoring, expect zero changes. Audit the overrides table.
