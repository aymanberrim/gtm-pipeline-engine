# Metrics

Twelve numbers describe the channel. Two are measured circuit breakers, one is a rule, nine are defaults replaced by the channel's own 8-week median. Definitions, formulas and the action when below in `skills/channel-operating-cadence/references/metrics-and-benchmarks.md`.

| # | Metric | Default benchmark | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verified-safe rate | 70% | weekly |
| 2 | Open rate | 35%, auto-pause below | daily |
| 3 | Click rate | 5% | weekly |
| 4 | Reply rate | 3%, auto-pause below | daily |
| 5 | Positive reply rate | 4% (1 in 25) | weekly |
| 6 | Signal-to-call within SLA | 90% | weekly |
| 7 | Reach rate | 30% | weekly |
| 8 | Qualified rate | 50% | weekly |
| 9 | Demos booked per week | north star, set by the CEO (default 3 for one operator) | weekly |
| 10 | Demo held rate | 80% | weekly |
| 11 | Cost per demo | under 10% of a demo's expected value | weekly |
| 12 | Safety-net count at 18:00 | 0 | daily |

Read together: reach × qualified gives the share of called leads that qualify (15% at the defaults). Forty signals a week is six qualified conversations and three to four demos, consistent with the north star. Cost per demo is computed from invoices and a timesheet, never from list prices; two days above zero on metric 12 blocks any scale-up.

## The CEO page

One page, every Friday: the five numbers (demos booked, demos held, positive reply rate, cost per demo, safety-net count), what changed since last week, what was fixed, what is next. Template and a filled example in `skills/channel-operating-cadence/references/ceo-weekly-report-template.md`.
