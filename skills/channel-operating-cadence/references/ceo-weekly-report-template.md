# CEO weekly report template

Purpose: one page, delivered the same day every week, that lets a CEO judge the channel in 3 minutes. Five numbers with last week beside them, then three short sections: what changed, what was fixed, what is next. Nothing else. The full 12-metric board exists and is linked, but it is not pasted in.

```
Leakiest step: name, actual rate against benchmark, leads behind it

- 
- 
- 

What was fixed (the one fix, and whether last week's moved its number)
- 

What is next (the one fix for next week, owner, date checked)
- 

Decisions needed from you (0 or 1)
- 
```

Rules for filling it:

- Every number has last week beside it. A number alone is a claim; a pair is a trend.
- "What changed" is facts, not effort: a segment paused, a pattern alert, a new objection heard twice, a domain that went amber. Never "worked hard on".
- "What was fixed" names one fix and says whether the previous one moved the number, with the number.
- "Decisions needed" is empty most weeks. When it is not, it is one question with the two options and the operator's recommendation.
- Delivered the same day every week, before the weekly review is 24 hours old. A late report is read as a bad week.

## A filled week, running example

The 40-person warehouse-robotics vendor from Shenzhen, entering Europe through the Netherlands, two resource posts live (a fleet-utilisation benchmark and a peak-congestion audit), one operator, one account executive.

```
Outbound channel, week 7, Netherlands

Five numbers                       this week   last week   benchmark
Demos booked (north star)            4           3           3
Demos held                           3 of 3      2 of 3      80 percent
Positive reply rate                  1 in 21     1 in 26     1 in 25
Cost per demo                        290         310         under 600
Safety-net count (end of day, max)   0           3 (Tue)     0

Funnel this week
Captured 62 > Enriched 55 > In ICP 39 > In sequence 220 (standing) > Signal 24 > Reached 11 > Qualified 6 > Booked 4 > Held 3 (one is next week)
Leakiest step: no funnel step is below benchmark this week. Signal rate is 24 of 220 in sequence, 11 percent against
the 8 percent benchmark; Signal to Reached 46 percent (benchmark 30), Reached to Qualified 55 percent (benchmark 50),
Qualified to Booked 67 percent (benchmark 60). The weak spot is per-segment: the 3PL sub-group replies at 2 percent,
half the channel's rate, and that is where the week's one fix goes.

What changed
- Third occurrence this month of the same incumbent fleet software as an objection, all at 3PLs: pattern alert
  raised, battlecard section on peak routing added.
- Demo held rate back to 100 percent after the day-before (T-1) confirmation was added to the email-link booking path.
- Second sending domain moved from amber to green after warm-up week 4; the 3PL segment resumes at 30 sends a day.

What was fixed
- Last week's fix (import script setting To qualify ICP without a date) held: safety net at 0 all five days.
- This week's fix: email 2 of the audit sequence now leads with the congestion-cost row for the reader's
  sector instead of the generic number. Target: click rate from 5 to 7 percent on 36 leads in sequence.

What is next
- Re-segment the 3PL group: those on the incumbent fleet software get the "what its routing cannot do in peak"
  angle; the others keep the congestion angle. Owner: operator, with segment-then-write. Checked next Friday
  on reply rate per sub-group after 60 sends.
- Germany: scorecard re-check before any second country, per the one-country-in-90-days rule.

Decisions needed from you
- None.
```

## What a bad report looks like, and why the CEO stops reading it

Twelve tiles pasted from the dashboard, no previous week. "Lots of activity on the German list." A list of six fixes started and none checked. A cost per demo computed from list prices. The north star described as "pipeline created" in currency instead of demos booked and held. A decision buried in paragraph four. Each of these costs the channel the CEO's trust faster than a bad week does; a bad week with the true numbers, one fix and a checked previous fix keeps it.

## Edge case: the week with zero demos

Report it as zero, with the funnel that explains it. Week 2 of the Austin AI-agent platform: Captured 41, Enriched 37, In ICP 22, In sequence 20, Signal 3, Reached 1, Qualified 0, Booked 0. The leakiest step is Sequence to Signal at 15 percent; the fix is the value email. "What is next" says so with a date. A zero with a cause and a fix reads as a machine warming up; a zero hidden behind "good conversations this week" reads as a channel that does not work.
