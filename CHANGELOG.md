# Changelog

All notable changes to this repo. Semantic versioning: MAJOR for a change in stage structure or skill identity, MINOR for a new skill or reference page, PATCH for fixes.

## 1.0.1

- Regulatory accuracy pass: CE stated as the Machinery Directive today with the Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 from 20 January 2027; GDPR transfer stated as the customer-to-vendor mechanism (DPF or SCCs Module 2), not the cloud provider's; NIS2 scope broadened and the Cyber Resilience Act added; works-council co-determination vs consent vs consultation distinguished; EU AI Act tiers corrected and a robot's safety-component AI treated as high-risk; every load-bearing date, article or tier now defers to counsel.
- Consistency pass: mobile enrichment removed from stage 2 (bought just in time at the call); Tier A defined as a core role at a buyer with proven or probable authority; Tier D routed to the stage-7 partner motion; worked-example arithmetic and word counts corrected; weekly review fixed at seven items and the CEO-report funnel made internally consistent; day-before (T-1) confirmation standardised.
- Voice: the repeated "What good looks like" template rephrased per skill.
- Validator now enforces `## Inputs`, `## The play`, `tags`, at least two reference pages, stage-README linkage, placeholder and em-dash scans across reference pages, and manifest de-duplication.
- README: reference case rewritten to separate the two measured campaigns and to note that downstream outcome numbers are the client's; added "What I'd need from you" and pointers to the worked message, filled CEO report and handoff pack.
